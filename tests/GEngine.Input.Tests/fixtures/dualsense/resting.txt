# DualSense USB input report, 64 bytes.
#
# CAPTURED FROM HARDWARE with examples/04-gamepad-probe.cs --capture
# model: DualSense (VID 054C, PID 0CE6) over USB, on Windows 11, 2026-08-26
#
# Nothing held, nothing touched, controller flat on a desk. Note bytes 1 to 4: the sticks
# report 7F 80 and 79 8B rather than 80 80 80 80. A real stick does not rest at the centre,
# and it wanders by a unit or two from report to report - which is the entire reason
# DeadZone exists, and why a game that trusts the raw axis drifts across the screen.
#
# Bytes 11 and after are the gyroscope, the accelerometer, the touchpad and the battery.
# They are live sensor data, they differ on every capture, and gengine does not decode them.
